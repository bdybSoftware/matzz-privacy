# Matzz Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026
**App:** Matzz (Google Play: `com.bindortyuzyirmibir.matzz`)
**Developer:** 1421Software. Contact: 1421feedback@gmail.com

Matzz is a math training app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device

Your exercise results (questions, answers, times and scores), your settings and your language choice are stored
**only on your device**. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes
them.

## Signing in (optional)

Matzz can be used without an account. If you sign in, your **email address** and password are processed by
**Firebase Authentication** (Google) to verify your identity. Your password is never visible to us. We don't use your
email for marketing.

To delete your sign-in account and its data, email **1421feedback@gmail.com** from that address with the subject
"Delete my Matzz account". We delete it within 30 days.

## What we don't do

- No ads, and no advertising ID.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
- We don't sell or share your data.

## Third-party services

- Firebase Authentication (Google), only if you sign in: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>

## Children

Matzz is a general-audience math practice app. It doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children. Signing
in is optional, and the app works fully without it.

## Changes

We'll update the effective date above when this policy changes.
