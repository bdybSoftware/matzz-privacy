# Matzz Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 10, 2026
**App:** Matzz (Google Play: `com.bindortyuzyirmibir.matzz`)
**Developer:** 1421Software. Contact: 1421feedback@gmail.com

Matzz is a math quiz app. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data stored on your device

Your quiz results (questions, answers, times and scores), your settings and your language choice are stored **only on
your device**. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

## No accounts

Matzz has no sign-in and no user accounts, and it doesn't collect your name or email address.

Earlier versions (up to 2.0.3) had an optional sign-in through Firebase Authentication (Google). If you created a
sign-in there and want it deleted, email **1421feedback@gmail.com** from that address with the subject
"Delete my Matzz account". We delete it within 30 days.

## What we don't do

- No ads, and no advertising ID.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
- We don't sell or share your data.

## Links

The app opens only this privacy policy and, if you choose to send feedback, your email app.

## Children

Matzz is a general-audience math app. It doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

We'll update the effective date above when this policy changes. A future version that adds ads will update this policy
before it is released.
